# Source policy

## Source hierarchy

1. User-provided artifacts and directly observed system state.
2. Normative specifications and official documentation.
3. Source repositories, release notes, and reproducible benchmarks.
4. Peer-reviewed or method-transparent research.
5. Independent operational reports with concrete evidence.
6. Community discussion for leads, not final authority.

## Community skill review

Before adopting a skill, inspect its full content, nested resources, scripts, license, provenance, update history, external calls, write behavior, and token footprint. Pin an exact revision. Test positive, negative, overlap, and adversarial prompts. Popularity is only a discovery signal.

## Certainty labels

- **Confirmed:** directly supported by current evidence.
- **Inferred:** conclusion follows from evidence but was not directly observed.
- **Unknown:** evidence is missing, conflicting, or stale.
