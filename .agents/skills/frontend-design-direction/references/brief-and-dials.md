# Brief and design dials

Capture:

- audience and desired perception;
- one primary user action;
- required sections and content priority;
- brand assets and forbidden changes;
- reference qualities to borrow, not pages to copy;
- target devices, accessibility needs, and performance budget.

Propose at most three distinct directions. For each, name the visual thesis, typography role, color logic, spatial rhythm, imagery strategy, motion level, strengths, and failure risks. Recommend one based on the brief rather than personal novelty.
